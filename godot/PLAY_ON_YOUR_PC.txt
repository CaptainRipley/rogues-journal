Rogue's Journal — run on your computer
======================================

You need Godot 4.7 (standard, not .NET):
https://godotengine.org/download/4.7.2/

1. Unzip this folder.
2. Open Godot 4.7.
3. Import / open the folder that contains project.godot.
4. Press Play (F5).

Click "Wake in the mud". Nix is the marsh fairy.
E take a gift / talk. F pickpocket her.

WASD move, mouse look, Space jump.

This is the ditch slice, not the full castle yet.
