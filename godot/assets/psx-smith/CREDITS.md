# The Closed Fist

The workshop is one textured building, not a closed stone box with flat-color tools in front of it. The street side of the forge bay is open. The player walks in under the roof. The shop rooms on the other side stay walled.

## Medieval Blacksmith

Daniel Andersson (Daniel74). CC0.

BlendSwap 75729, also on OpenGameArt: https://opengameart.org/content/medieval-blacksmith

The BlendSwap license in the zip is Creative Commons Zero. Textures are packed in that blend (brick, iron, wood, roof tiles, lava in the forge, and the rest). They are resized to 512 px on the long side so they sit with the town's nearest-filter PSX look.

`workshop.glb` is that building, floor included, with the open forge bay facing the street. The anvil, forge, bellows, grindstone, barrels, workbench, tool rack, and chimney are the meshes that shipped in the blend. The yard copies those barrels, the log piles, and the woodcutter's axe out to the street corner and the side of the shop. Clay pots, the timber fence across the lane, and the mud, ruts, and puddles are built in the scene from the town's existing ground textures. `mud.png` is nearly black, so the street copy lifts that floor into wet brown before it is tinted. That dirt is one ribbon along the S, the same width from the ditch to the gate. The shoulders are broken wet dirt, stones, and weeds, not a mown grass curb. The other Harth lots keep a short fence and a small cluster of these props off that path. Their upper floors, stall walls, and corner posts reuse this blend's plaster, brick, and bark. The shop sign is painted in the scene and hung from an iron arm on the street gable, same low-res lettering as the other lots. It is not a texture from this blend.

`strike_hammer.glb` is the hammer from the same author's CC0 Medieval Smith Pack (https://opengameart.org/content/medieval-smith-pack), with the wrought-iron and wood textures taken from the blacksmith blend above. Marta swings it on a loop while she stands at the anvil.

## Not placed

The earlier flat-color exports from the smith pack (anvil, forge, bellows, and the rest) are no longer in the scene. The Kenney quench barrel is replaced by the barrels in the building. Quaternius swords remain in `godot/assets/models/` and are not required on this lot; the building's own tool rack is on the wall.
