# Rogue's Journal

Working title. A first-person medieval RPG told as a rogue's private journal.

The king took your wife. You are going to his feast. How you get there — steel, shadow, silver tongue, or spark — is the game.

**Tone:** dark and gritty, with dry comedic undertones. Mud, torchlight, bad wine, worse nobles.

**Status:** pre-production / vertical slice
**Play the slice:** open `prototype/index.html`

## Design pillars

1. **First person means the page.** You never see your face. You see what the rogue sees.
2. **Approaches are verbs, not classes.** Mix them. A fight can start with a lie.
3. **The wife is a person.** She is not a trophy.
4. **Comedy lives in the margins.** The world is cruel. The journal notices how stupid cruelty looks.
