# Story bible — Rogue's Journal

## Locked premise

- The king took your wife. Not in a ballad. In a cart, with soldiers, on a market morning.
- He is throwing a feast to show the court he can take what he wants.
- You are going. You write as you go.
- You choose how: steel, shadow, tongue, spark — and mixes.

## Working names

| Role | Name | Note |
|---|---|---|
| You | Unnamed in UI. Journal voice only. | Can later ask for a name. |
| Wife | **Mara Venn** | Weaver. Sharper than the king. |
| King | **Aldric the Generous** | Court nickname. He is not. |
| Kingdom | **Harth** | Small, wet, proud of its wine. |
| Castle | **Redhollow** | Stone the color of old blood after rain. |
| Ally | **Sister Pell** | Chapel clerk who hates the king for tax reasons. |
| Comic relief | **Hob** | Toll-keeper. Will sell anyone. |

## What actually happened

Aldric saw Mara at the wool market and decided a gap in his life should be filled by someone who would look good next to a roast swan. Mara went because the alternative was the square.

She has been in Redhollow nine days. She has mapped the servants' stair. She has made Sister Pell like her. She is not waiting to be a song.

The player believing they are the only plan is useful. It is also the joke.

## Act I beats (prototype)

1. Empty house. Rain. Loom still threaded.
2. The road. Mud. A hanged WELCOME sign.
3. Hob's toll.
4. The inn "The Generous Cup."
5. Outer wall. End of slice.
