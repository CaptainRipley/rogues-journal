# Tone & look — Rogue's Journal

The world is a wet boot. The journal is the only honest thing in it. Sometimes honesty is funny.

## Visual

- Palette: soot, dried blood, brass, wet slate, sick candle gold. One warm color per scene.
- 16-bit feel. Nearest-neighbor scale. No blur.
- Cheap lighting. Faces live in a cone of torchlight.
- UI: parchment over stone. Ink shakier as Nerve drops.
- Comedy in the picture is small: a stupid chin on a portrait, a swan slightly on fire. Never wacky fonts.

## Voice

Write like a person who has slept in ditches and still notices fabric.

Do: short sentences when scared; one precise ugly detail per page; asides in parentheses.
Don't: modern slang; fate-had-other-plans; making light of the kidnapping itself. The king can be ridiculous. The act is not a gag.

## House style

> The loom is still threaded. She would hate that. I hate that I noticed the thread before the door.

> They named the inn after him. The ale tastes like a policy.
