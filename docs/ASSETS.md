# Asset targets — Rogue's Journal

Use original work or licensed packs. No rips.

## In the Godot scene

- Opening woods: PS1 Haunted/dreamer Nature Pack — see `godot/assets/psx-nature/CREDITS.md`
- Town humans (Hob, Marta, Bren, Cole, Pell, Ralf): Nocturnal Watch PSX characters (CC0, toboas). The GLBs have no skeleton, so the scene swings arms and legs from the stand pose — see `godot/assets/psx-characters/CREDITS.md`
- The Closed Fist is Daniel Andersson's CC0 medieval blacksmith (textured, open forge bay). Its barrels, logs, and axe dress the Harth yards. The street is one wet-dirt ribbon along the S, with a worn verge of dirt, stones, and weeds. The other lots are timber, stone, and thatch built from the Going Medieval modules plus the smith's plaster and bark — see `godot/assets/psx-smith/CREDITS.md` and `godot/assets/psx-buildings/CREDITS.md`
- Shop boards and street lamps are built in the scene: hanging shields on iron arms, low-res lettering and trade marks, iron cages, and additive ground pools so night reads in GL Compatibility without a separate mesh pack
- Nix stays the `nix.png` billboard

Want dark castle / wet village, not sunny farm tiles.
